Privacy

This site is run by Kikai LLC. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or ads, and has no forms.

The theme button remembers ink or paper in your browser’s local storage, under the name kikai-theme. It never leaves your device, and clearing this site’s data removes it.

Our web host, GitHub (GitHub Pages), processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site, under GitHub’s privacy statement.

If you email us, your message passes through ImprovMX, our mail forwarder (its privacy policy), and is stored in Gmail. We use your name, email address and message only to reply and keep a record of our conversation. We never sell it or add you to a mailing list.

Ask us at hello@kikaihq.com to see or delete what we hold.

We don’t track you across sites, so Do Not Track signals change nothing here.

If this changes, we’ll update this page and its date.
